DISCOVER THE DIFFERENCES

Less guesswork. More adventure.

Sample guides based on the server’s public information. Final content will be reviewed with the Inferna team.

🎯

Tasks & Bosses

Turn hunting objectives into tokens for boss encounters.

How to start

Accept up to three tasks through the client. Rewards are tokens, not XP. Boss access is through the Edron depot teleport. Tasks require Premium.

Official guide ↗
⛏️

Mining

Gather ores and progress towards rarer materials.

How to start

Buy a Beginner’s Pickaxe from a Tools NPC for 200 gp. Bronze requires skill 0; Silver requires 15. Better tools improve efficiency, but can break.

Official guide ↗
🪓

Woodcutting

Explore forests and gather wood for your recipes.

How to start

Craft the first axe using 10 Bronze Ingots, 5 Silver Ingots and 1 Hatchet. Start with Birch Trees and unlock other trees as your skill grows.

Official guide ↗
⚒️

Crafting

Bring mining and woodcutting together to create tools and materials.

How to start

Use an Iron Hammer and Anvil to forge materials. Wood refining uses a Woodworker’s Knife and Cutted Tree Trunk. Each recipe has a Crafting requirement.

Official recipes ↗
🌿

Herbalism

Harvest food and develop another profession.

How to start

Blueberry Bushes offer a starting option in the world. Harvesting develops your skill. Mango Trees and Mushroom Pots also exist, with their own acquisition and use conditions.

Official guide ↗
🔥

Attributes & Overcharge

Understand equipment bonuses and temporary power.

What changes

Equipment can have up to five attributes. Orbs modify bonuses. Overcharged Items last two real-time hours after first activation; the timer continues while offline.

Official guide ↗
