# Expanded historical quest review — 13 October 2026

The research covers the 98 candidate entries. The published result contains 70 quest guides and one separately labelled renewable collection. 27 candidates remain withheld; this does not assert that they were all absent from original 7.4.

## Research and evidence limits

A fresh fetch checked the 98 corresponding Portuguese Wiki addresses: 88 successful responses, representing 64 distinct pages because several older quest names now redirect to grouped walkthroughs. Their extracted article bodies were unchanged from the previous walkthrough comparison. Ten legacy titles returned 404; alternative references already recorded in the editorial inventory were consulted where available. Searches also covered English TibiaWiki version tables and spoilers, TibiCam recordings, Tibia Stories historical articles, early forum results, GameFAQs, a version-selectable wiki and reconstruction-server lists.

Neither an item's introduction date, a parent page's earliest date nor a server advertising “7.4” proves the presence of each reward container in the original client. Reconstruction lists contain post-7.4 content such as Port Hope, Sam's Old Backpack and Explorer Society; these were not adopted as release evidence. A 2004 GameFAQs guide lacks its promised quest sections and was not used to admit any quest. Registration dates beside forum posts were not treated as post dates. Search snippets alone were not used to establish introduction dates.

The version-selectable wiki's Behemoth page, despite its 7.4 label, contains Cyclops Smiths, Fierce Berserk and Divine Caldera in its instructions. Those instructions are unsuitable as original-7.4 evidence.

## Corrections and checks

- Triangle Tower: external lever reference corrected from 32574,32122,7 to 32574,32121,7; missing combat creatures added. Invisible creatures and escape risk remain relevant; Stealth Ring is an option rather than a universal safety guarantee.
- Noble Armor: retained the Skjaar sequence `hi → key → yes → yes → redips → 7 → black → yes`, 1,000 gp and Key 3142, separate from the level-35 door. Rewards are Noble Armor and Crown Helmet. Each answer must wait for the preceding question.
- Iron Helmet: identified as respawn, excluded from the one-time quest planner and completion UI. Old-map area marker and current Wiki coordinate differ; the guide discloses that difference instead of asserting an exact original SQM or respawn timer.
- Behemoth: retained level 80 for 7.4; clarified that 60 is an earlier/adapted requirement. Prepared players for the historically described four Behemoths, two Dragons and two Priestesses, rather than later Cyclops Smiths.
- Crusader Helmet: combat list now includes Giant Spiders and the dwarven enemies attested by a 7.3 recording. Breakable barriers were added in 7.8 and the Sam's Old Backpack extension is later; neither is an original-7.4 requirement.
- Annihilator: explicitly states one choice among four rewards and that the bear comes inside Present Box. A retrospective article's wording suggests a replacement; the item's individual Wiki dates Annihilation Bear to 7.24, so the catalogued bear was retained, not replaced with an ordinary Teddy Bear.
- Edron's old chest collections remain distinct from Against the Spider Cult (8.1). Original Giant Smithhammer and Skull of Ratha collections remain distinct from Explorer Society (7.6). Explorer Brooch remains withheld because its item date conflicts with the old quest-table date.
